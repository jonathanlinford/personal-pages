Gather in 2027

Before the Salt Lake Temple reopens for good, the public gets six months to walk through it. That has not happened since 1893. Good excuse to get the whole family to Utah for a few days.

The idea

The plan, in one sentence

Everyone comes to Utah. One morning we all walk through the temple together. The rest of the days are a family reunion at a rental somewhere good. The open house is free, open to anyone, and does not require membership, so it works for the whole family. Below are three ways to build the trip, with real places to stay and the dates that fit each one.

What to tell everyone coming. Anyone can go. You do not have to be a member, and there is no ticket cost. You walk the inside of the temple at your own pace with guides, in about an hour. Dress is just respectful and comfortable, nothing special. Kids are fine. Because it is free and low-key, it is an easy thing to invite people to.

Three ways to do it

Pick the one that sounds like your kind of trip

Each is sized for about 15 to 20 people on a mid-range budget, and the temple morning fits into all three. The places listed are real properties in each area that sleep the whole group. Nightly ranges are 2027 estimates, worth checking when you actually book.

Summer about 2.5 hrs north of the temple
01
Bear Lake

Lake days

Bear Lake is the turquoise one, shallow and warm enough to actually swim in, with sandy beaches and the raspberry shakes the town is known for. Kids in the water most of the day. The temple sits right on the drive up from Salt Lake, so you do the open house on the way in or out.

Boat and paddleboard rentals · Rendezvous Beach · the raspberry shakes · Raspberry Days festival, early August · Minnetonka Cave

▸ A sample four days
Day 1Everyone lands in Salt Lake. Temple open house late morning, lunch on Temple Square, then drive north to the lake house for dinner.
Day 2Beach and boats all day. Big group dinner, bonfire.
Day 3Minnetonka Cave in the morning, shakes, lake games, family photos at golden hour.
Day 4Slow morning, pack up, easy drive home.
Spring, summer or fall about 45 min from the temple
02
Midway / Heber Valley · easiest logistics

One basecamp

Heber Valley is a green bowl about 45 minutes from Temple Square, so the temple is a short morning drive and you are back for lunch. One house, everyone together, no packing up mid-trip. This is the pick if you want the least driving and the most time under one roof.

Spring or fall temple first, then about 4 hrs south
03
St. George / Zion · the road trip

Red rock

Make it a road trip. Spend a night or two near Salt Lake for the temple and Temple Square, then drive about four hours south to St. George: warm days, resort pools, and Zion nearby. Best in spring or fall. Midsummer down there runs past 100 degrees, so avoid July and August.

Day 3Zion for the day: shuttle, Emerald Pools, the Riverside Walk.
Day 4Sand Hollow or Snow Canyon, pool afternoon, a show at Tuacahn that night.
Day 5Slow morning, fly out of St. George or drive back to Salt Lake.
