Which one for you

  • Just want lights on/off at set times → Honeywell Econoswitch. Set up to 7 on/off events per day, repeat daily or weekly, plus a sunrise/sunset astronomic mode and a random "looks lived-in" vacation mode. No hub, no app. This is the direct answer to the programmable-timer ask.
  • Want auto on/off from motion, cheap and simple → Lutron Maestro. Dumb but bulletproof; no hub, no neutral. Weakness: PIR can shut lights off if you sit still (reading, at a desk).
  • Want presence detection + dimming + Home Assistant automations → Inovelli Blue mmWave. The one you subscribed to. mmWave beats PIR at the "I'm here but not moving" problem, and it can also do time-based schedules through Home Assistant, so it can technically do everything the other two do, at 2-5x the price and with a hub + neutral wire required.

Backstory: the Inovelli mmWave is "Project Linus"

The Inovelli mmWave switch is codenamed Project Linus. In June 2022, Linus (Linus Tech Tips) posted "I've been defeated… 'Smart' lighting in my new house" about his smart-lighting struggles. Inovelli was his #2 pick for the build but lost the deal because they had no motion/presence switch at the time. In response, Inovelli built exactly that switch, named the effort after him, announced it on the WAN Show, and launched it on Indiegogo.

Current status: It made it through certification and into production; Blue Series (Zigbee) units air-freighted to the California warehouse in Nov 2025 and started reaching backers around Dec 2025. It's now for sale on the store at $99. The Red Series (Z-Wave 800) version has trailed behind. You signed up on the Blue/Zigbee + Home Assistant list back in Feb 2025, but there's no order/payment on file, you were on the updates list, not a backer.

Before you buy, check the box

  • Neutral wire: both the Inovelli and the Honeywell need a neutral in the box. Your Visionary Homes build almost certainly has this. The Lutron does not need one.
  • Zigbee hub: the Inovelli needs a Zigbee coordinator (e.g. a SkyConnect/Sonoff dongle on Home Assistant via ZHA or Zigbee2MQTT). The other two are standalone.
