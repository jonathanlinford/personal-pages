Premium membership face-off
Robinhood Gold VS SoFi Plus

Two fintech subscriptions that promise to pay for themselves. One is a $50-a-year brokerage upgrade, the other a $120-a-year banking bundle. Here's where each one actually earns its fee.

rates & terms verified 2026-06-10

TL;DR

Robinhood Gold is the easier yes. At $50/yr it pays for itself with either ~$1,500 of idle cash (3.35% APY vs basically nothing on the free tier) or any meaningful IRA contribution (3% match vs 1%). Max a $7,500 IRA and the match alone is $225/yr, which is 4.5x the fee. The 3% flat cash-back Gold Card rides along at no annual fee.

SoFi Plus got harder to love on March 31, 2026, when SoFi killed the free direct-deposit path and made it $10/mo for everyone. The catch: direct deposit still gets you 3.10% savings APY and all the loan discounts without Plus. You're paying $120/yr mostly for +1.40% APY on your first $20K (max $280/yr), 5% grocery cash back on their new Smart Card, and the 1% invest match.

They're different tools. Gold upgrades a brokerage; Plus upgrades a bank. If you only pick one and you invest at all, Gold's math is far more forgiving. Plus only pencils if you keep a big savings balance at SoFi or spend heavily on groceries with their card.

01 / THE CONTENDERS

What you're actually buying

Robinhood Gold
$5/mo
or $50/yr (2 months free) · first 30 days free

A premium tier on Robinhood's brokerage. Almost every perk is investment-flavored: yield on cash, retirement match, cheaper leverage, better data.

  • 3.35% APY on uninvested brokerage cash, no cap (free tier: ~0.01%)
  • 3% IRA match on contributions (free tier: 1%); up to $225/yr at the $7,500 limit
  • Gold Card: 3% cash back on everything, $0 annual fee, no foreign transaction fees
  • First $1,000 of margin interest-free; discounted rates after
  • Bigger instant deposits (up to 3x portfolio value)
  • Cheaper derivatives: futures $0.50/contract, index options $0.35/contract
  • Research stack: Morningstar reports, Nasdaq Level II, Cortex AI insights
  • Extras: $500 closing credit via Sage Home Loans, Strategies fee capped above $100K
SoFi Plus
$10/mo
$120/yr · no annual option · no free path since 2026-03-31

A premium tier on SoFi's banking super-app. The perks sprawl across savings, cards, loans, investing, and a pile of partner discounts.

  • 4.50% APY on savings, first $20K only (3.10% above that; direct deposit without Plus already gets 3.10%)
  • 5% grocery cash back on the new SoFi Smart Card (Plus members only)
  • 10% cash-back boost on the classic SoFi card (2% becomes 2.2%)
  • 1% invest match on eligible recurring deposits (5-year hold) + 1% crypto buy match
  • 5% back on SoFi Travel bookings (except air)
  • Unlimited sessions with credentialed financial planners; IPO allocation priority
  • Loan discounts: 0.25% off personal loans, 0.125% off student refi, $500 off mortgage origination
  • Partner grab-bag: 20% off TurboTax, $50 Rakuten bonus, Vivid Seats & Seated credits, CNBC+ and Care.com discounts, SoFi Stadium perks
⚠️
The March 2026 rug-pull: until 3/30/2026, SoFi Plus was free with any eligible direct deposit. As of 3/31/2026 it's $10/mo for everyone, full stop. Direct deposit still unlocks the 3.10% savings APY and the lending-rate discounts on its own, so the real question is whether the Plus-only extras are worth $120/yr on top of what direct deposit already gives you.

02 / CATEGORY BY CATEGORY

Head-to-head

Each round compares the premium tier against what you'd get from the same company for free, because that's the only honest way to score a subscription.

💵 Yield on cash

SoFi, with an asterisk
Robinhood Gold
3.35% APY, uncapped, on uninvested brokerage cash (FDIC-insured via partner-bank sweep). The free tier earns essentially nothing, so the entire 3.35% counts as a Gold benefit. $10K parked = ~$335/yr.
SoFi Plus
4.50% APY but only on your first $20K in savings; 3.10% above that. Direct deposit already earns 3.10% without Plus, so the Plus premium is really +1.40% on $20K max = $280/yr. Checking stays at 0.50% either way.
Scoring note: SoFi's headline rate is higher, but Robinhood's delta over its own free tier is much larger. If your cash lives at a 4%+ HYSA elsewhere anyway, neither APY perk should drive the decision alone.

💳 Credit cards

Robinhood
Robinhood Gold Card
3% flat cash back on every category, $0 annual fee, no foreign transaction fees, Visa Signature perks, virtual card numbers, free authorized users of any age. APR 21.99% to 29.99% variable. Still gated behind an application queue, and the 3% requires keeping Gold active.
SoFi cards + Plus
New Smart Card: 5% on groceries (Plus only); classic SoFi card gets a 10% boost, 2% to 2.2%. 5% groceries is excellent for a big grocery budget, but it's one category; everything else trails the Gold Card's flat 3%.
Scoring note: a 3% flat card is top-of-market and quietly the strongest single perk in either bundle. SoFi's 5% grocery niche can out-earn it only if groceries dominate your card spend.

🏦 Retirement & investing

Robinhood
Robinhood Gold
3% IRA match (vs 1% free) = up to $225/yr on a maxed $7,500 contribution; that's $150/yr more than the free tier, triple the annual fee by itself. Strings: stay subscribed 1 year, leave the money in the IRA 5 years. Plus cheap futures/index options and the Cortex/Morningstar/Level II research stack.
SoFi Plus
1% match on eligible recurring net deposits into taxable invest accounts (5-year hold) and 1% crypto buy match. Unlimited certified financial planner sessions is genuinely valuable if you'd otherwise pay for advice. IPO access is a niche perk.
Scoring note: 3% on an IRA beats 1% on taxable deposits for almost everyone without a workplace plan; the match is on contributions you were (hopefully) making anyway.

🏠 Loans & borrowing

Tie / depends
Robinhood Gold
First $1,000 of margin free, then discounted rates (around 5% on balances up to $50K as of early 2026). $500 closing-cost credit through Sage Home Loans. Margin perks only matter if you actually borrow against your portfolio.
SoFi Plus
Extra 0.25% off personal loans, 0.125% off student refi, $500 off mortgage origination, $200 off home equity. Real money if you're borrowing, but direct deposit gets you these same discounts without Plus, so they're barely a Plus benefit at all.

🎁 Lifestyle & partner perks

SoFi
Robinhood Gold
Thin on lifestyle: a rewards program with partner perks and experiences, plus the card's Visa Signature benefits (trip interruption protection, etc.). Gold is unapologetically an investing product.
SoFi Plus
The kitchen sink: 20% off TurboTax Federal, $50 Rakuten welcome bonus, $20 Seated dining reward, 10% off Vivid Seats (max $50), 40% off CNBC+, Care.com year for $99, HelloPrivacy and Trust discounts, SoFi Stadium expedited entry. Each is small and partner-fulfilled, but a TurboTax filer + one ticket order can claw back $50 to $80 of the fee.

05 / READ THIS BEFORE PAYING

Fine print & gotchas

Robinhood Gold

  • IRA match handcuffs: keep Gold for 1 year and the matched assets in the IRA for 5 years, or the match can be clawed back.
  • The card is a leash: the Gold Card's 3% only flows while Gold is active; cancel and the card's whole premise collapses.
  • Queue for the card: still an application queue; funded brokerage + active Gold required, approval not guaranteed.
  • APY drift: Gold cash APY fell from 5.00% (2024) to 3.35% (mid-2026), tracking Fed cuts. The perk shrinks as rates fall.
  • Carry APR: 21.99% to 29.99% variable on the card; one carried balance wipes out a year of rewards.

SoFi Plus

  • You may already have the good parts: direct deposit alone keeps 3.10% APY and all lending discounts. Plus is only the delta on top.
  • $20K APY cap: the 4.50% headline rate stops at $20K; larger balances fall back to 3.10%, capping the perk at ~$280/yr.
  • 5-year hold on matches: the 1% invest match forfeits if you pull funds early or lapse the qualifying criteria.
  • Partner-fulfilled perks: most lifestyle benefits are run by third parties, change without notice, and require activation legwork.
  • Precedent of repricing: SoFi already converted a free benefit into a $120/yr fee once (March 2026). Terms say benefits "are subject to change."
06 / VERDICT

Who should buy what

Get Robinhood Gold if…

$50/yr · pays for itself fast

…you contribute to an IRA at all (the 3% match is the single best dollar-for-dollar perk in either program), keep more than ~$1,500 idle in your brokerage, or want a no-annual-fee 3% flat cash-back card and don't mind the subscription leash.

Skip it if your cash and retirement accounts live elsewhere and you'd never use the card; the research perks alone aren't worth it.

Get SoFi Plus if…

$120/yr · needs real usage to pencil

…SoFi is already your primary bank with $9K+ parked in savings, or you'd put serious grocery spend on the 5% Smart Card ($350+/mo covers the fee by itself vs a 2% card), or you'll actually book the planner sessions and harvest the partner perks.

Skip it if you just want good APY and loan discounts; plain direct deposit still delivers those for free.

My take

For our situation the order is clear: Gold first, Plus only with a job to do. With no workplace 401(k), every retirement dollar goes through an IRA, and a 3% match on a maxed contribution is $225/yr against a $50 fee. That's a 350% return on the subscription before counting cash yield or the card.

SoFi Plus is the inverse: the two things I'd actually want (high savings APY, loan discounts) are still free with direct deposit. The honest case for Plus in a household our size is the 5% grocery card; at a realistic family grocery budget it covers the $120 and then some, but that means adopting the Smart Card as the daily driver. Worth a look if we ever move primary banking to SoFi; not before.
